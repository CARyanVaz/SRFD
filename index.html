What We Do

Core Capabilities & Services

Every service is field-tested across Maharashtra and backed by ICAI-grade documentation, digital tools, and measurable community outcomes.

📊

Social & Community Research

Core Competency

End-to-end primary and secondary research across rural and urban communities — from survey design through final report. We capture the ground truth that policy requires.

  • Household-level demographic & socio-economic surveys
  • Census studies, baseline & endline evaluations
  • KAP (Knowledge, Attitude, Practice) studies
  • Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) & In-depth Interviews
  • Public opinion polling & community needs assessments
  • Rapid Rural Appraisals (RRA) and PRA methods
📄 Deliverables: Survey instruments, CAPI data, cleaned datasets, executive summary, full research report
🏛

Consultancy & M&E Advisory

Strategic Services

We help government bodies, NGOs, and CSR wings design, monitor, and evaluate social programmes with rigour — turning field data into actionable intelligence.

  • Theory of Change (ToC) & Logical Framework (LogFrame) design
  • Programme Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) frameworks
  • Third-party evaluation (TPE) for government schemes
  • Social Return on Investment (SROI) analysis
  • Institutional capacity assessment & gap analysis
  • Project Design, Implementation Support & Reporting
📄 Deliverables: M&E framework, quarterly MIS reports, evaluation reports, ToC diagrams, PIPs
💻

Tech & Digital Solutions

Digital Transformation

Custom-built digital tools that put real-time field data at your fingertips — from mobile data collection to live dashboards and community management portals.

  • ODK / KoboToolbox CAPI survey tool design & deployment
  • Custom web portals for beneficiary management
  • Interactive Power BI / Tableau dashboards
  • GIS mapping & spatial data visualisation
  • Database design, management & automation
  • WhatsApp & SMS-based community outreach systems
📄 Deliverables: Live dashboard, mobile app, geodata maps, automated MIS, API integration
🎓

Education & Skill Development

Capacity Building

Structured capacity building that goes beyond training rooms — equipping field workers, community leaders, and youth with skills that translate into real employment and income.

  • Vocational Training Programmes (VTP) for youth & women
  • Field investigator & enumerator training
  • SHG (Self Help Group) capacity building workshops
  • Financial literacy & digital awareness programmes
  • Career guidance & placement linkages
  • Content development: modules, workbooks, handbooks
📄 Deliverables: Training manuals, pre/post assessments, attendance records, placement data

Livelihoods & Employment

Community Development

Sustainable livelihood models designed from the ground up — connecting beneficiaries to schemes, markets, and opportunities through targeted facilitation and follow-through.

  • Beneficiary identification & profiling for government schemes
  • MGNREGA, PM-Kisan, PMEGP, NRLM linkage facilitation
  • Market linkage & value chain support for farmers and artisans
  • SHG formation, handholding & credit linkage
  • Micro-enterprise incubation & business planning
  • Employment camp organisation & placement drives
📄 Deliverables: Beneficiary registers, scheme saturation reports, livelihood outcome tracker

Knowledge Products

Translating raw research into publication-ready knowledge products that influence policy, inform academia, and give implementing agencies the evidence they need.

  • Thematic compilations on health, agriculture, gender, water

About SRFD

Social Research Foundation & Development

Social Research Foundation and Development (SRFD) is a registered sole proprietorship based in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), Maharashtra — rooted in the heartland of the Marathwada region and serving communities across India.

Our core strength lies in combining rigorous social science methodology with modern digital tools. Where most research organisations stop at data, we continue through to action — building systems, training communities, facilitating schemes, and publishing findings that genuinely move the needle on social outcomes.

We work with Central and State Government departments, UN agencies, international and domestic NGOs, academic institutions, and corporate CSR teams who need credible, field-verified social intelligence and implementation support.

The People Behind the Work

Leadership & Core Team

SRFD is led by a multidisciplinary team combining entrepreneurial vision, medical community intelligence, and deep anthropological field expertise.

Founder & Promoter
Balaji Chakrawar
Founder MA Social Science

Balaji Chakrawar is the founding force behind SRFD — the vision, the drive, and the operational backbone of everything the organisation does. As Promoter, he identified the critical gap between raw grassroots data and actionable social policy, and built SRFD to bridge it.

With over 18 years of professional experience in social research, Bandu Baburao Chakrawar has held key leadership and supervisory positions—such as State Coordinator, Project Manager, Quality Assurance Consultant, and Senior Field Coordinator—for major national surveys and health initiatives supported by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), UNICEF, and UNFPA. Having successfully managed more than 52 CAPI and paper-based projects, including landmark studies like the National Family Health Survey (NFHS), the Longitudinal Ageing Study in India (LASI), and the District Level Household and Facility Survey (DLHS), he possesses deep expertise in large-scale field team management, rigorous data quality monitoring, survey administration, and master training.

His entrepreneurial instinct shapes SRFD's ability to move fast in the field — commissioning surveys, mobilising field teams, forging partnerships with government departments and NGOs, and translating community intelligence into programmes that work. He brings to the organisation an on-the-ground understanding of Marathwada's social landscape that no textbook can replicate.

Under his leadership, SRFD has grown from a local research initiative into a credible multi-district research and development organisation serving government, NGO, and CSR clients across Maharashtra.

Co-founder
Dr Mithilesh Varma
BAMS & MPH

Dr Mithilesh Varma holds a Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS) — a five-and-a-half year professional degree that combines classical Ayurvedic science with modern biomedical training including anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology, and clinical practice. He also holds a Master of Public Health (MPH), which strengthens his ability to translate clinical insight into practical public health research and programme design.

This dual grounding — in traditional Indian systems of medicine and in evidence-based clinical assessment — gives Dr Varma a uniquely integrative lens for community health work. He understands how rural and peri-urban populations in Maharashtra access, experience, and interpret healthcare, and how formal health schemes interact (or fail to interact) with existing traditional health practices.

At SRFD, Dr Varma leads health-sector research, ensuring that studies on nutrition, maternal health, ICDS, and primary healthcare are designed with clinical precision and interpreted with genuine field sensitivity. He brings the practitioner's perspective to survey instruments, FGD guides, and policy briefs — bridging the gap between data and lived health realities.

Domain Expertise

Research Director
Dr P. Swarup
PhD — Population Studies

Dr P. Swarup holds a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Population Studies — one of the most rigorous qualitative social science disciplines, encompassing social structures, ethnography, kinship systems, and the study of human societies across time and context.

Anthropology as a discipline trains its practitioners to go beyond numbers: to embed in communities, understand belief systems, decode social hierarchies, and surface the unspoken factors that determine whether a programme succeeds or fails. Dr Swarup brings exactly this depth to SRFD's research portfolio.

His expertise shapes the organisation's qualitative methodology — FGD design, ethnographic observation, community mapping, and the interpretation of socio-cultural data that purely quantitative methods would miss. He ensures that SRFD's research is not just statistically valid but anthropologically honest — reflecting the actual complexity of the communities it serves. He also provides academic rigour to publications, policy briefs, and knowledge products, lending SRFD's work credibility with universities, think tanks, and government research bodies.

Domain Expertise
Ethnographic Field Research Qualitative Methodology Social Structure Analysis Cultural & Tribal Studies Academic Publications Policy Research Design
Research Director
Dr Akash Wankhede
PhD — Population Studies 25 years experience

Dr. Akash Wankhede is a distinguished social research professional serving as a Senior Research Officer for the NFHS project at the International Institute for Population Sciences (IIPS), Mumbai. His career features leadership roles in major national initiatives, including State Coordinator for UNICEF's SWABHIMAAN project, Senior Project Officer for NFHS-4 and NFHS-5, Project Coordinator for DLHS-4, and Programme Manager for DLHS-3.
